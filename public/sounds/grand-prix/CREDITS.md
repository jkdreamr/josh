# Grand Prix sounds

## commentary/

The commentary booth (James and Sophie), recorded with the Chatterbox neural text to speech model by
`scripts/grand-prix-commentary.py` from the lines in `src/os/arcade/games/grand-prix/lines.ts`. The voices come from
the reference clips in `scripts/grand-prix-voices/`, which were made with the Kokoro voices `bm_fable` (James) and
a blend of `bf_emma` and `bf_alice` (Sophie), so no real person's voice is cloned. The clips carry Chatterbox's
inaudible Perth watermark.
