# Grand Prix sounds

## commentary/

The commentator's lines, recorded with the Kokoro neural text to speech model (voice `bm_fable`) by
`scripts/grand-prix-commentary.py` from the lines in `src/os/arcade/games/grand-prix/lines.ts`.

Kokoro-82M, model and voices: Apache License 2.0, https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
