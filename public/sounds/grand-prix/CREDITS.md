# Grand Prix sounds

## commentary/

The commentary booth (James and Sophie), recorded with the Kokoro neural text to speech model by
`scripts/grand-prix-commentary.py` from the lines in `src/os/arcade/games/grand-prix/lines.ts`. James is the
`bm_fable` voice; Sophie is a blend of `bf_emma` and `bf_alice`.

Kokoro-82M, model and voices: Apache License 2.0, https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
